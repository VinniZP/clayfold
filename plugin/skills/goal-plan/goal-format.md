# MISSION.md format for a goal

`MISSION.md` lives in the goal's workspace root and grounds the plan. Write it in the learner's language.

```md
# Goal: {Goal name}

## Why
{1–3 sentences: the outcome and who gains from it. "My clients book sessions without messaging me", not "learn to make apps".}

## First version
- {What the first version does, observably: "a client picks a free slot and gets a confirmation"}
- {…at least two}

## Constraints
- {Hours per week, deadline, devices, budget}

## Already know
- {Experience as the learner stated it, with depth: "made a website in Tilda, never wrote code"}

## Stack
- {Only when the learner named one}

## Out of scope
- {What the first version leaves out}
```

- Write the headings in the learner's language and keep their order. The first heading keeps the form `# <the word "Goal" in the learner's language>: <goal name>`: the server reads the goal name from the text after its colon, and shows the first bullet of the second `##` section.
- Keep it under one screen.
