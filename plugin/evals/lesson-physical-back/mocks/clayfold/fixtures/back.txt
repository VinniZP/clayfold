=== src_back_exercises
These exercises stretch the lower back. Do them on a mat or a firm surface. Move slowly and stop if any movement makes your pain worse.

Knee to chest stretch. Lie on your back with your knees bent and both feet flat on the floor. Bring one knee up towards your chest and hold it with both hands just below the knee. Keep the other foot flat on the floor and your lower back pressed gently into the floor.

Hold the stretch for 20 to 30 seconds while breathing normally. Slowly lower the leg and repeat with the other leg. Repeat 2 to 3 times on each side.

You should feel a gentle stretch, not pain. Do not bounce or pull hard on the knee. If you cannot reach your knee comfortably, hold the back of your thigh instead.

Pelvic tilt. Lie on your back with your knees bent. Tighten your stomach muscles and flatten your lower back against the floor. Hold for 5 seconds, then relax. Repeat 10 times.

=== src_back_safety
Most lower back pain gets better within a few weeks. Keeping active and doing gentle stretches can help.

Stop the exercise and get advice from a doctor or physiotherapist if you have pain that spreads down your leg, numbness or tingling in your legs, or pain that gets worse during the exercise.

Get urgent medical help if you have numbness around your bottom or genitals, problems controlling your bladder or bowels, or back pain after a fall or injury.
